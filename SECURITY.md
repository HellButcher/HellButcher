# Security Policy

## Scope

Use this repository to report security vulnerabilities for:

- **All repositories under my GitHub account** (*HellButcher*)that do not specify their own `SECURITY.md` file.
- **All services hosted on `hommel.dev`** or subdomains of it.

## Reporting a Vulnerability

If you discover a security vulnerability, please report it to us privately. **Do not open a public issue for security vulnerabilities.**

### How to Report

Please submit your report directly through GitHub's Private Vulnerability Reporting:

**[Report a vulnerability via GitHub Security Advisories](https://github.com/HellButcher/HellButcher/security/advisories/new)**

### What to Include in Your Report

To help me to resolve the issue as quickly as possible, please include:

- A description of the vulnerability.
- Steps to reproduce the issue (a Proof of Concept is highly appreciated).
- The potential impact of the vulnerability.
- The specific repository, project or service affected (ideally a specific URL pointing to it).

### Preferred-Languages

Please only communicate in German or English with me.

### Our Commitment

Once I receive your report, we will:

- Acknowledge receipt of your report promptly.
- Investigate the issue with priority.
- Keep you updated on our progress and the steps taken toward a resolution.
- Credit you for your discovery (if desired) once the issue is resolved and made public.

  There will be NO financial reward!

### Disclosure Policy

We follow a **responsible disclosure** model. We ask that you give us sufficient time to address and patch the vulnerability before making any information public. Publicly disclosing vulnerabilities without prior notification may hinder our ability to protect users and services.
